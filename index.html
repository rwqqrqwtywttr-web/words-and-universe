Behind The Words

"माझ्या शब्दांत जपलेल्या काही खास आठवणी आणि अनुभव"

Welcome to Words & Universe. I am Prince Chautmol. Writing has never been just a craft for me—it is a spiritual journey of translating the unexpressed whispers of life into enduring language.

Through my essays on relationships (नाती), human empathy (माणुसकी), motherly love (आई), equality for the specially-abled (दिव्यांग), and the existential realities of life, my goal is to give every reader a quiet sanctuary of reflection. Every word has a universe waiting to be discovered.
